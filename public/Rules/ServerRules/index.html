UNIT - Server Rules

UNIT's moderators reserve the right to issue warnings, sanctions or greater actions on a case-by-case basis under reasonable circumstances. The 3 warning system may be bypassed at their discretion. Attempting to evade punishment by leaving the server will result in a permanent ban.

If you wish to dispute an unfair sanction issued to your account or dispute a sanction for somebody else, you may forward these concerns by opening a ticket. However, publicly complaining and making a larger issue about action taken against your account will lead to further punishment.

Offense Classes

  • Class I “Minor” offense [I]: A verbal warning is issued, if not done by bad faith and you do not have a history of violating this rule in the past more than a few times then a warning will not be issued. Failure to comply with staff instructions will result in the RV to be subject to rule A-1 and in repeat offenses of A-1, the RV will be subject to A-NULL.
  • Class II “Moderate” offense [II]: A warning is issued, and possibly a timeout, though a verbal warning could be given if not done by bad faith and this is the first time you've violated this rule. Failure to comply with staff instructions will result in the RV to be subject to rule A-1 and in repeat offenses of A-1, the RV will be subject to A-NULL.
  • Class III “Severe” offense [III]: Two warnings are issued, and possibly a timeout. If deemed to be done by bad faith, you will be subject to A-NULL. Failure to comply with staff instructions will result in the RV to be subject to rule A-1 and in repeat offenses of A-1, the RV will be subject to A-NULL. This warning is unappealable.
  • Class IV “Terminal” offense [IV]: Subject to a ban, no questions asked.

Notes

  • RV = Rule Violator
  • Any rule with 2 classes applied falls to the moderators discretion on an by incidence basis. This can however be challenged/changed by a Senior Moderator or Head Moderator
  • Depending on the situation the chat may be purged after an incident
  • Lower case i (like A-2-i) are for numericizing, upper case I within square brackets (like [II]) are for denoting offense classes.
  • All rules apply outside of their specific category.
  • RVs violating Class-I or Class-II rules outside of UNIT will not be prosecuted unless a user outside/inside of UNIT reports them
  • RVs violating Class-III or Class-IV rules outside of UNIT will be prosecuted with the same punishment as if it was committed within UNIT boundaries.
  • Recruits get one warning and then a ban.
  • Multiple violations at the same time will stack.
  • UNIT operates on a 3 composite warning system, don't be lenient on your attitude in the server, the moderators won't hesitate to strike you down, with more severe infractions resulting in more warnings.
  • UNIT will not punish anyone who breaks Severe or less rules outside of the faction.

Section A: Discord Rules

A-NULL (A-∅): Unacceptable Behavior [IV]

Unacceptable behavior is defined as actions or sentences that make others feel uncomfortable or the repeating of violating rules.

A-1: Refusing Staff Instructions [I/II]

Refusing staff instructions is defined as disobeying lawful instructions given by a superior officer, Moderation Team, or OCM.

A-2: Disrespect [I/II]

Disrespect is defined as showing extreme hatred or dialkenes to another member within official UNIT channels.

A-3: Excessive Profanity [I/II]

Excessive profanity is defined as repeated use of profanity during unwanted/unneeded situations.

A-4: Unauthorized Advertisement [I/II]

Unauthorized advertisement is defined as the use of messages or images to draw people to a certain thing outside of UNIT with the sole intent to gain more members/views without the permission of the Oversight Committee.

A-5: English [I/II]

Breaking the English rule is defined as having conversations in a different language that isn't modern English.

A-6: Drama [I/II]

Breaking the drama rule is defined as instigating or participating in any form of drama within official UNIT channels, please take it to DMs.

A-7: Unauthorized Mass Ping [I/II]

Unauthorized mass pings are defined as pinging a certain role more than 3 times without a valid reason or pinging everyone.

A-8: False Reports [II]

False reports are defined as opening tickets to report a RV without substantial evidence.

A-9: Fraud [II/III]

Fraud is defined as forging falsified documents, statements, or images for self benefit or intent to cause chaos.

A-10: Spam [II/III]

Spam is defined as sending unnecessary messages repeatedly through official UNIT channels.

A-11: Impersonation [II-IV]

Impersonation is defined as masking yourself as someone with the intent to deceive others so that you in a way have an advantage, as otherwise from your normal status.

A-12: Punishment Evasion [III]

Punishment evasion is defined as using methods to gain access to UNIT material or channels in order to bypass a restriction placed on you from a punishment.

A-14: Harrassment [III/IV]

Harassment is defined as constant abuse of power or negativity to a specific user or group.

A-15: Ear Rape [III]

Ear Rape defined as sudden extreme loud noise without warning.

A-16: Suggestive Text [III/IV]

Suggestive text is defined as any form of communication, with hostile intent, hinting at NSFW/Discriminative language.

A-17: Treason/Unauthorized Leaking [III/IV]

Defined as any action that aims to damage UNIT. Or the sharing of UNIT documents without OCM approval.

A-18: Discriminative language [III/IV]

Discriminative language is defined as language or actions that impose hate or extreme negativity to a group or individual users.

A-19: NSFW Imagery [IV]

The sharing of NSFW imagery in UNIT channels or UNIT members DMs.

A-20: NSFW Text [III/IV]

Sending NSFW texts in UNIT channels or UNIT members DMs.

A-21: Malicious Links [IV]

The sharing of malicious links with the intent to do harm.

A-22: Server Raiding [IV]

Planning, participating or performing a server raid.

A-23: Serious Threats [IV]

Making serious and damaging threats towards anyone.

A-24: Phishing [IV]

Attempting with success or failure to phish someone.

A-25: Unauthorized Privacy Violation/Doxing [IV]

The sharing of someone’s personal information.

A-25: PL5 Blacklist [IV]

If you are found to be on the PL5 blacklist document.
