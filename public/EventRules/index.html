UNIT - Event rules

(Only available in English)

Please note that rules and restrictions can be added to the event or altered by the event host’s discretion, so ensure you listen carefully during briefings. Server rules apply in events too

Additionally, mistakes can happen - but please ensure you learn from them.

Ensure that when joining an event late or leaving an event early you inform the host, whether it be in dms or by joining the VC they are in to tell them

Communication

Communications are to be done through our Discord radio and messaging channels. Avoid using ROBLOX chat due to age restrictions.

Try to use clear, easy-to-understand callouts (contact front, enemy right side, enemy five o'clock, helicopter south west)

DO NOT:

  • Scream useless info
  • Talk over the squad leader or anyone else
  • Spam comms incessantly

Discipline

Follow orders

Never question orders*

No lone wolfing (going on your own) unless permitted by the squad leader

Stick with your squad (unless otherwise stated by the squad leader)

Do not troll/grief

Do not take off apparel (shirts, pants, etc.)


*Do not obey an unlawful order (i.e. if your squad leader is trying to do something that goes against special event rules/restrictions laid out by the event host)

Regulations

Ensure that all of our rules and regulations are read carefully and followed. If they are not followed, you will be asked to cease doing whatever is wrong or change/remove whatever is incorrect and by failing to to this you will receive a sanction(s).

Firing Weapons

Do not fire in FOB

Use single shots or short bursts for precise and accurate shots and to save bullets.

Avoid the use of automatic firing mode unless providing suppression (like with an MG or vehicle turret)

Joining and leaving events

Ensure that the event host knows when you join or leave an event, or else you will not receive your points.

Unless stated in the event callout, assume all events are free to join at any point.

Vehicle spawning

Always ask for permission from the event host or your squadleader to spawn a vehicle in. Ask every time you want to spawn in a vehicle unless they tell you otherwise.
