UNIT - Server Rules

(Only available in English)

Upon joining the UNIT discord server, you agree to abide by our server rules. Failure to comply will result in a punishment ranging from Warnings, Demotions, Point deductions/resets or Bans depending on the severity of the violation committed.

Each user (yourself included) is provided 3 warnings before a permanent ban. We greatly encourage that you review our rules as we want to keep our community clean and any form of disrespect, harassment, or misconduct on behalf of the user is met with an appropriate punishment without exception. We expect maturity and respectful conversation between our users - if this isn't what you like to hear, we strongly encourage you to find a community that fits your preference.

Rule 1: Respect

Respect everyone in the community including any individuals or groups of people. Harassment, discrimination, hostility, toxicity and political discussion are not tolerated and users who persist after being told to stop will be met with appropriate action. Threats, doxxing, sexual abuse or mistreatment even if in DMs will lead to an immediate ban.

Rule 2: No discrimination

Discriminatory language (slurs like the n-word, the fa-slur and the r-word) and statements are strictly prohibited. This includes attempts to bypass filters (using symbols, spaces or alternative spellings) and applies across text, media, voice, nicknames and statuses. Context such as jokes or quotes is not an excuse.

Rule 3: No harmful media

Media (memes, videos, images, gifs etc.) that is intended to harass, offend or harm any group based on ethnicity, religion, sexuality, political leanings or medical conditions (including epilepsy is strictly prohibited. This includes media that promotes violence, hatred, or discrimination against any individuals or group of people. Any user who posts this type of media will be met with appropriate punishment depending on severity. Users are not allowed to post media containing their own faces or those of their family, friends, or any associated individuals.

Rule 4: No spamming

Spamming channels with any form of material, including text, images, pinging or other media is prohibited. Playing or spamming extremely loud audio in VC using soundboards, mic presets, voice changers, or any similar means is also prohibited.

Rule 5: No advertisement

Advertisement and solicitation related to other factions, services, communities, products, social media accounts, or other similar forms of media is prohibited. This also includes DM advertising.

Rule 6: Use channels properly

Please adhere to the rules and topics assigned to each channel, which can be found in places like the channel description and pinned messages. Misusing channels is prohibited. Memes that relate to a channel's specific topic or memes that are relation to on-topic conversations are permitted.

Rule 7: No malicious products

Malicious links, downloadables, phishing attempts, doxxing, or any other harmful activities will result in an immediate and non-negotiable ban. Discord crashers of any kind will also be subject to an immediate ban. Making threats related to these actions, even as a joke, is prohibited.

Rule 8: No server raiding

Any form of server raiding, including association with, encouragement of or participation in, will result in the entire raiding party being permanently banned without any warning. This action is non-appealable.

Rule 9: No NSFW/NSFL

NSFW/NSFL explicit or shock material (gore, graphic war-footage, self-harm, pornography etc.) including Profile Pictures, in any form, is prohibited. Suggestive content (such as sexual language, overly-revealing characters, sexually suggestive conversations and innuendos) and excessive or discomforting conversations is prohibited. Discussion of alcohol and drugs is also prohibited.

Rule 10: Follow staff's instructions

When you are instructed to cease & desist a certain activity or conversation by a staff member you are required to comply. Refusing to do so will result in an appropriate sanction and possible escalation.

Rule 11: English only

Due to moderation concerns, this is an English-only server. Use English in all text and voice channels.
